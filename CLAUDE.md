# Nederdam Bouw — website

- **Huisstijl is leidend:** read `docs/huisstijl.md` before any visual or copy change. It records the KADER brand board: five exact brand colours (charcoal #0E0E0E, bronze #B08B6F, stone #C9C2B8, olive #4A5A46, taupe #3A3A36, plus paper #F4F0E8 from the logo files), Söhne + Canela (stand-ins: Hanken Grotesk, Newsreader), the logo rules and the graphic elements. Online version: https://claude.ai/artifact/JcV8kyjAY2DY495Pe2Fssi.
- **Logos:** only the supplied files in `public/brand/`, rendered through `src/components/chrome/Logo.tsx`. Never redraw, approximate or recolour a mark.
- **No invented facts:** no made-up projects, reviews, numbers, addresses or certifications, and no stock photos presented as Nederdam work. Missing content is **hidden, not shown as a placeholder**; image positions show architectural drawings until real photos exist. Track what is missing in `docs/content-status.md`.
- **Web interpretation of the huisstijl:** light (paper) ground, charcoal used strategically; primary button bronze with charcoal text; the grotesk for ~90%, the serif for at most one statement per page. See `docs/brand-identity.md`.
- **Branch:** work on `claude/vakvorm-discovery-architecture-rbcju2`; bring changes to `main` only when the owner asks for it.
