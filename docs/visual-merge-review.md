# VAKVORM — Visual Warmth & Commercial Energy Pass

An art-direction refinement (not a redesign). Structure, grid, compositions, ServiceIndex,
navigation and the typography *system* are unchanged. Goal: warmer, richer, more inviting and
more commercially confident — while staying premium, architectural and distinct. Reference
contractor screenshots were used for **atmosphere only**.

## What felt too dusty before
- Palette was one cool, dry note (cool bone + cool blue-charcoal ink). Little tonal depth.
- The accent had been stripped almost entirely, so nothing felt warm or alive.
- CTAs were dry/technical (a thin bordered ghost); nothing read as *inviting*.
- No integrated trust layer — credibility was implied, never reassuring.
- Leftover mono on the brand descriptor + hero eyebrow read as a developer interface.

## Inspiration taken from the references (atmosphere, not components)
- More **warmth** in the neutrals.
- A **deep feature colour** for contrast/gravitas (they use navy; we use warm espresso — more
  architectural, and keeps us distinct).
- A **restrained warm accent** on the primary conversion action (they lean gold; we use muted
  clay, once).
- **Integrated trust** and a clearer invitation to make contact.
- More **tonal rhythm** between sections.

## What was deliberately NOT copied
Header utility/badge bars; "known from TV" / 5-star promo styling; Google-review widgets;
trust-icon rows; rounded service cards; heavy gold branding; stat tiles; busy testimonial
stacks; serif display; salesy landing-page behaviour. No fabricated numbers/ratings/awards/
guarantees.

## Colour refinements
- Warmed the whole palette: `--bone` → warm sand, added `--sand` (deeper warm neutral for
  alternating sections), warm `--paper`, warm near-black `--ink`.
- Replaced the cool dark with **`--deep` warm espresso** for feature sections (statement,
  contact, footer) — richer, warmer gravitas.
- Warm **clay** accent (`--oxide` #a85a34) — restrained; visible only on the conversion CTA and
  interactive states. Not gold, not loud.
- **Tonal rhythm:** bone → sand (trust) → bone → espresso (principle) → bone → sand (werkwijze)
  → espresso (contact/footer).

## CTA refinements
- Conversion CTAs ("Offerte aanvragen", contact panel) now **warm clay, filled**, slightly
  softer corners (`--radius-cta` 6px) and a restrained `↗` — inviting and desirable, still one
  accent only.
- Primary/secondary buttons softened to 6px, warm hover. Hero primary now invites contact
  ("Vrijblijvend kennismaken") rather than routing to the (currently empty) projects page.

## Trust / conversion refinements
- New **trust band** (warm sand, hairline-separated, no icons/badges/numbers): *Eén
  aanspreekpunt · Complete begeleiding · Heldere planning · Hoogwaardige afwerking* — truthful
  value props placed right after the hero for early reassurance.
- Contact panel now warmer (espresso + clay CTA); footer/contact hierarchy unchanged but
  benefits from the warmer palette.

## Typography (unchanged system, small application fix)
- No system change. Completed the earlier fix: **brand descriptor** and **hero eyebrow** moved
  from IBM Plex Mono to **Public Sans** (descriptor keeps a light 0.12em lockup tracking; eyebrow
  is sentence case). Mono is now reserved for genuine spec/index/classification (capability
  strip, triad, numbers). No return to orange-word / overtracked-label AI tricks.

## Shape language
- Buttons `6px`, media frames `5px` — subtly softer for warmth. Structural containers stay
  near-square (`2px`). The site remains architectural and controlled.

## How Vakvorm stayed distinct
Espresso (not navy), one muted-clay accent (not gold), datum/spec language, ServiceIndex,
near-zero cards, calm editorial composition — none of the reference's contractor-template
signatures were imported.

## Still waiting on photography
The media wells are still placeholders. The warmth now lives in palette, CTAs, trust and
rhythm; **real photography is the remaining lever** and will bring the biggest lift in warmth
and liveliness. Layout is art-directed to receive it.
